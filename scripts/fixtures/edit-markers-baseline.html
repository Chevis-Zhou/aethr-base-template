Why Meridian

What Working Here Is Actually Like

The differences that show up in year two, not in the pitch deck.

  • Capped at sixty households

    The book is closed at sixty by design, so the advisor who wrote your plan is the one who answers the phone.

  • Fee-only, disclosed in writing

    No commissions, no revenue sharing, no proprietary products. The fee schedule is on the engagement letter and nowhere else.

  • Equity comp as the default case

    ISOs, RSUs, QSBS and secondary sales are the normal work here rather than an exception handled by a specialist elsewhere.

  • Plans you can re-run

    Every projection ships with its assumptions written down, so you can test what happens when one of them turns out wrong.
