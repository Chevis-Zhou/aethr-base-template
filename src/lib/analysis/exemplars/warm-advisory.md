# Register — warm advisory

Second person. Emotional-outcome headlines. Question-shaped eyebrows.

**Fits:** small-practice where the buyer is an individual, not an institution.
**Exemplar:** Arca Wealth (#3 in CMS order).
**Source:** delivered copy captured from the live site 2026-09-04 — `arcawealth.com`.

---

## A caveat that belongs on this file specifically

`productized-voice.md` §6 records that running the banned-vocabulary list across the
delivered corpus produces **real hits on this site** — "an ecosystem we nurture",
"Comprehensive care", three flagged words in two lines. That is not an argument that the
list is wrong; it is the argument for the rail, because the list governs generated copy only
and must never be run against copy a client wrote or approved.

An exemplar is different from a rail field, though: it is the thing generation imitates most
directly. Copy imitated from a flagged line would be blanked by the copy floor a moment
later, which teaches nothing and wastes a field. **The flagged lines are therefore excluded
from the excerpts below**, and this note stands in their place so a later session does not
"restore" them as an omission.

Everything below passes the floor as written.

## Hero

> **COMPREHENSIVE WEALTH MANAGEMENT**
>
> **This is what wealth is supposed to feel like.**
>
> From advisors who aim to see the full picture of who you are, and champion what you've
> built.

The headline is about a feeling and makes no claim that could be checked, which is exactly
right for the register and exactly what the floor's rules are shaped to permit. Note what it
does *not* say: no number, no superlative, no credential.

## Service descriptions — the emotional-outcome form

> **Retirement Planning** — Retirement should feel like an exhale. Beneath it sits one quiet
> question: is the money enough? We answer it clearly and keep answering it as life changes,
> so you can settle into these years knowing they're yours.

> **Equity Compensation** — Your equity holds years of work and real possibility, along with
> decisions that rarely come clearly: when to exercise, what to hold, how the taxes land. We
> stay ahead of each one, so what you've earned translates cleanly into the life ahead.

> **Estate Planning** — Your wealth is meant to outlast you, and to land exactly where you
> intend. We carry that responsibility with the same intention and care you brought to
> building it, so what you leave behind is clarity and protection rather than questions.

> **Business Exit Planning** — Selling your business turns years of work into something new:
> the freedom to decide what comes next. We plan well before the sale, protecting the value
> you created so that the next chapter, whatever you make it, is fully open to you.

> **Tax Planning** — Tax strategy here isn't a once-a-year exercise but a continuous thread
> through how you build and keep your wealth. We plan ahead across your whole financial
> life, protecting more of what you've earned so more of it stays invested in the life and
> legacy ahead.

Three sentences each, on a fixed frame: name the reader's situation in their terms, name the
hard part, then say what the firm does about it and what that buys. The service is described
by the feeling it resolves, never by its mechanics.

## Feature descriptions

> **CONFIDENCE** — Whether you're planning for retirement or making sense of your stock
> options, you understand the choices ahead and which path fits the life you're building.
> The complexity is ours. The decisions are yours.

> **FREEDOM** — The time you'd otherwise spend managing it all, given back to you. The work
> of your financial life happens in the background, so your time goes to your family, your
> aspirations, and the life you're actually living.

One-word feature titles in caps. The two-fragment close — *"The complexity is ours. The
decisions are yours."* — is the register's signature rhythm.

## FAQ answers

> **Are Arca's advisors fiduciaries?** Yes. Every Arca advisor is a fiduciary, legally and
> ethically bound to act in your best interest, always. As fiduciaries we will always
> recommend products that suit you best, so your plan is shaped by your goals alone, not by
> what pays us.

> **Is there a minimum to work with Arca?** Arca's minimum is typically around $500,000 in
> investable assets, though it's a guideline more than a hard line. If you're not quite
> there today, it's still worth a conversation. What matters more is fit: your stage of
> life, what you need from an advisor now, and where you're headed.

Answers the question in the first three words, then explains. The second answer volunteers
the softening qualifier before the reader has to ask for it, which is what makes the register
warm rather than merely polite.
