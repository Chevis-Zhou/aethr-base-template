# Register — institutional advisory

Third person plural. Full-sentence headings. Credentials-forward. No urgency.

**Fits:** small-practice — firms, funds, advisories, clinics.
**Exemplars:** Totipotent Capital (#1), Johnson Capital (#6), HauerX (#8).
**Source:** delivered copy captured from the live sites 2026-09-04 —
`totipotent.vc`, `johnsoncapital.com`, `hauerx.com`.

Generated fields only. Numbers, credentials and named people are rail content on a real
build; they appear below only where they are inseparable from the sentence shape being
shown.

---

## Totipotent Capital — the primary exemplar

### Hero

> **Investing at the inflection point of healthcare innovation.**
>
> Totipotent Capital backs early growth-stage healthcare and life sciences companies with
> demonstrated traction, investing at the point where risk is reduced and the path to scale
> becomes clear.

The headline is a full sentence and names the thesis, not the category. The subheadline
does the whole job in one sentence: who they back, at what stage, and the condition that
defines the stage.

### Eyebrow and heading pairs

| Eyebrow | Heading |
|---|---|
| INVESTMENT FOCUS | We invest in healthcare and life sciences companies where technology, clinical insight, and system-level alignment create the conditions for durable growth. |
| WHY TOTIPOTENT | Totipotent Capital combines venture discipline with deep healthcare operating expertise, enabling differentiated sourcing, underwriting, and portfolio support. |
| REPRESENTATIVE INVESTMENTS | Our portfolio spans diagnostics, clinical workflow optimization, therapeutics, women's health, digital health, and enabling healthcare infrastructure. We back companies that have moved beyond concept and are demonstrating real traction. |

Eyebrows are set in caps and are two or three words of pure navigation. The heading carries
the entire claim as one long, precise sentence — the register tolerates a heading that
would be too long anywhere else, because precision is the thing being sold.

### Feature titles and descriptions

> **Disciplined Entry Point** — We invest after early uncertainty has been reduced, focusing
> on companies with demonstrated traction and clear pathways to scale.

> **Healthcare System Alignment** — We prioritize businesses aligned with reimbursement
> structures, regulatory dynamics, and value-based care trends.

> **Hands-on Expertise** — Our perspective is informed by clinical, operational, legal,
> regulatory, and policy experience across complex healthcare environments.

> **High-Conviction Portfolio Construction** — We invest selectively, support our strongest
> companies actively, and reserve capital for follow-on opportunities.

Each description is one sentence beginning with "We" and a concrete verb. No description
claims a result; each describes a practice.

### Service descriptions

> **Diagnostics & Lab Services** — Enabling earlier, more accurate clinical decision-making
> and expanding access to high-quality diagnostics across care settings.

> **Optimizing Drug Discovery & Delivery** — Advancing more efficient, targeted approaches
> to therapeutic development while reducing time, cost, and uncertainty.

> **Longevity & Wellness** — Supporting companies improving quality of life, extending
> healthspan, and serving underpenetrated markets in preventive and consumer health.

### CTA band

> **FOR INVESTORS** — Explore Fund II, our investment strategy, and how we partner with
> aligned investors.
>
> **FOR FOUNDERS** — Learn what we look for, how we work with management teams, and whether
> Totipotent may be the right fit.

Two audiences, addressed separately, each told what they will find rather than what to feel.
"whether Totipotent may be the right fit" is the register's characteristic move: it offers
the reader the option of leaving.

## Johnson Capital — the same register, shorter sentences

### Eyebrow and heading pairs

| Eyebrow | Heading |
|---|---|
| Who We Are | (body-led section, no heading) |
| Expertise | Real-Time Intelligence. Decades of Relationships. |
| Our Process | Eight Stages, One Outcome |
| Client Success | Real Relationships. Real Results. |
| The Briefing | Updates & Insights |

Eyebrows Title Case, headings built from two short balanced clauses. Where Totipotent uses
one long sentence, Johnson Capital uses two fragments in parallel — same register, faster
cadence.

### Section body

> Founded in 1987 and relaunched in 2025 as an independent, tech-forward advisory platform
> built for speed and execution. We structure debt and equity solutions across multifamily,
> industrial, office, retail, and specialized real estate asset classes.

> Real-time visibility into where lenders are actively deploying capital, paired with a
> network built over nearly four decades: originators and credit officers who now sit in
> senior seats across the lender universe.

> From initial engagement to closing, our process is built for speed, clarity, and certainty
> at every stage.

### Service descriptions — the compressed variant

> **Bridge Financing** — Short-term capital for transitional assets
> **Construction Financing** — Ground-up and major renovation capital
> **Permanent Financing** — Long-term debt for stabilized assets
> **Mezzanine Debt & Preferred Equity** — Subordinate capital that stretches leverage
> **Joint Venture Equity** — Aligned capital partnerships for growth
> **Recapitalizations & Loan Workouts** — Strategic options when the plan changes

Noun phrases, no verbs, no full stops. A valid alternative to Totipotent's full sentences
when the list is long and the reader is scanning.

## HauerX — the register at its most clipped

### Hero

> **GROWTH FOR WHAT'S NEXT.**
>
> HauerX buys, backs, and builds AI-native companies that help leaders decide which
> innovation & growth bets to make and move the right ones into market.

### Eyebrow and heading pairs

| Eyebrow | Heading |
|---|---|
| AN AI-NATIVE PORTFOLIO COMPANY | GROWTH FOR WHAT'S NEXT. |
| The Portfolio | AI-native companies that turn ambition into compounding advantage. |
| The Proof So Far | AI-native, installed, producing. |
| What We Build | AI-native companies spanning the enterprise growth continuum. |
| From the Serial Growth Lab | Insights on AI-native growth. Practical. Evidence-based. No hype. |

### Service descriptions — the one-line question form

> **Growth** — How businesses find, win, and keep customers.
> **Intelligence** — How businesses see what's happening and what's coming.
> **Innovation** — How businesses build what's next.
> **Operations** — How businesses run faster every day.
> **Strategy** — How businesses decide where to go and how to get there.
> **Experience** — How businesses show up to the world.

Six parallel sentences on the identical frame. Repetition of structure is the point; varying
it would cost more than it bought.

### CTA band — segmented by reader

> **FOR B2B COMPANIES** — Pipeline harder than it should be? You sell into the Fortune 500
> and the old playbook is slowing down.
>
> **FOR ENTERPRISES** — Ready to move faster? Rethinking how you operate, innovate, or
> govern AI?
>
> **FOR OWNERS** — Thinking about what's next for your company? If you're considering your
> next chapter, I'm always up for a confidential conversation.

Question-shaped headings addressed to a named reader. Note the first person singular in the
owner block — the founder speaking as himself, in a site otherwise written in third person
plural. It is used exactly once, where the offer is personal.
