# Register — practice credibility

Third person about the subject. Eyebrow + Title Case heading pairs. Proof-dense.

**Fits:** solo-professional — tutor, coach, therapist, consultant.
**Exemplar:** Maxematics (#4 in CMS order).
**Source:** delivered copy, `Business/clients/max-maxematics/MaxTutoring/mockup/content.json`.

Generated fields only. Testimonials, differentiator lines, rates and the pull-quote are
verbatim-rail content the client wrote, and are excluded — imitating a client's own words
is not what an exemplar is for.

## Eyebrow and heading pairs

| Eyebrow | Heading |
|---|---|
| Why Maxematics | The Maxematics Difference |
| Meet Max | Meet the "Max" in Maxematics |
| Topics | One Tutor, Many Topics |
| Word on the street | What People Are Saying |
| Rates | Simple, Tailored Sessions |
| Giving back | The Gen. H Norman Schwarzkopf Scholarship |
| Contact | Start the Conversation |

The eyebrow names the section in the plainest possible words. The heading is where the
personality goes, and it is a phrase rather than a sentence throughout — this site is the
corpus outlier on capitalization, and it shipped well.

## Meta description

> Maxematics is Max Schwarzkopf's private tutoring practice in Tampa, FL — one-on-one
> sessions across 12 topics, including SAT/ACT and Desmos support, virtual or in person.

Name, person, place, what happens, the qualifier. No adjective that could be argued with.

## About copy

> Max started tutoring with the idea that academic success is found at the intersection of
> preparation and attitude. While subject mastery is the obvious goal, Max aims to do that
> while also inspiring a shift in thinking that makes learning fun.

> Beyond tutoring, Max is a STEM student, researcher, rower, escape room aficionado, foodie,
> community contributor and campus leader.

Two short paragraphs. The first is the practice's idea of itself; the second is the person.
The list in the second paragraph is unranked and slightly unserious, and that is what keeps
the page from reading as a CV.

## Section body copy

> Max's grandfather spent his life in service — to his country, and to the young men and

> teaching calculus and mechanics at West Point. The scholarship exists in his memory,

## Contact intro

> Tutoring inquiries, scholarship questions, and scheduling — send a note and Max will
> reply. Availability is limited, so a little detail about the student and the goal helps.

Says what to send and why it helps. Does not ask twice.
